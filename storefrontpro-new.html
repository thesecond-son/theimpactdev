Questions, answered

Before you book.

Can I keep selling on WhatsApp and Instagram?

Yes. Keep every channel you use now. The store gives those conversations somewhere to end: a product page where the customer pays.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. You get a 1-hour live training plus recordings, and post-launch support: 30 days on Standard and Growth, 3 months on Scale. If you can post on Instagram, you can add a product.

Can customers pay by bank transfer?

Yes. Paystack is built into every package and takes cards, bank transfers and USSD, with instant confirmation.

Can I receive international payments?

Yes, from the Growth Store upward: Paystack International, Stripe, Square or PayPal (subject to eligibility), with multi-currency prices and shipping zones.

Will I keep paying you after the store is built?

The package fee is one-time. After the included period, hosting is ₦11,500 per month (or ₦33,200 per quarter, ₦119,500 per year), the domain renews at ₦25,000 per year from year two, and maintenance is optional at ₦10,000 per month. Payment providers charge their own fee per transaction.

How long does it take?

Standard and Growth stores go live within 10 business days of receiving your content. Scale takes 4 to 5 weeks.

What if my product photos and details aren't ready?

That's normal. On the consultation we'll tell you exactly what we need and agree a realistic timeline. Clear phone photos are fine to start.

Why does Scale start "from" a price?

It depends on your catalogue, integrations and fulfilment. We agree the exact scope and price with you on the discovery call.
