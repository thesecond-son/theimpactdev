Questions, answered

Everything you may want to know before you get started.

Can I continue selling through WhatsApp and Instagram?

Absolutely. Your online store doesn't replace your existing sales channels. You can continue using WhatsApp, Instagram and other platforms to reach customers while directing them to your store to browse products, place orders and make payments.

Do I need technical knowledge to manage my store?

No. Every store comes with a 1-hour live training walkthrough plus tutorial recordings you can watch again whenever you need. You also get post-launch support: 30 days on Standard and Growth, and 3 months on Scale.

Can I manage my products and orders myself?

Yes. Every order, payment and delivery detail sits in one dashboard, and your stock updates itself as items sell. Scale Store also lets you give team members their own logins with the right level of access.

Can customers pay through bank transfer?

Yes. Paystack is built into every package and accepts debit cards, bank transfers and USSD, with instant confirmation.

Can I receive international payments?

Yes, from the Growth Store upward. It adds Paystack International, Stripe, Square or PayPal (subject to eligibility), plus multi-currency pricing and custom shipping zones for different regions. The Standard Store is built for local payments.

Will my online store work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every page, product and checkout step is designed phone-first.

What happens if I need help after my store launches?

Every package includes a defined post-launch support period. During this period, you can contact our team for assistance within the agreed support scope. We also offer an optional Growth Partner Maintenance Plan for businesses that want continued technical assistance after their included support period.

Will I keep paying you after the store is built?

Your package fee is a one-time fee for building the store. A few ongoing costs follow, and we show all of them in the cost breakdown above the packages: hosting keeps your store online (₦11,500 monthly, ₦33,200 quarterly or ₦119,500 annually after your included hosting period), your domain renews at ₦25,000 per year after the first year, and the optional Growth Partner Maintenance Plan is ₦10,000 per month after your support period. Payment providers also charge their own fee on each transaction.

What if I don't have all my product information ready?

You can discuss your current level of preparation with our team during the consultation. We'll explain the content, product information and business materials required before development begins, and agree on a realistic project timeline.

How long does it take to launch?

Standard and Growth stores go live within 10 business days of receiving your content. Scale Store takes 4–5 weeks from receipt of your content.

Will you help me set up my store?

Yes. We build it, test it and hand it over fully working, with training, your Google Business Profile setup, a launch flyer and an Ecommerce Marketing Guide. For accounts billed directly by providers, such as payments, WhatsApp messaging or couriers, we'll guide you through setting each one up.

Why does Scale Store start "from" a price?

Scale Store is scoped after a discovery conversation, so the final price depends on your catalogue, integrations, fulfilment requirements and the scope of the work. Third-party fees, such as APIs, WhatsApp, email and subscriptions, are separate. The consultation is where we agree the exact scope and price with you.
