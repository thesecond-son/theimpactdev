The problem

You may have antivirus, firewalls, backups and password policies. Your people still make the final decision.

Technology can only do so much. Consider what happens when:

  • An employee receives an email that looks exactly like it came from the CEO.
  • Someone sends your accountant a new bank account and asks for an urgent payment.
  • A staff member clicks a malicious link or opens the wrong attachment.
  • A colleague shares confidential information without realizing the risk.
  • Someone notices something suspicious and has no idea what to do next.

The employee doesn't need to be careless or malicious. They just need to make one wrong decision at the wrong time.

Financial risk

Fraud, ransomware and payment manipulation.

Information risk

Client data, company data, credentials and confidential documents.

Business risk

Downtime, reputational damage, lost trust and interrupted operations.

A security incident can cost far more than preventing the mistake in the first place.
